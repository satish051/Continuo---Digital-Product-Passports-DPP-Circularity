The Circular Loop

Designing for the Next Lifecycle

A Digital Product Passport doesn't just track data—it actively enables a circular economy by connecting stakeholders at every stage of a product's life.

1. Eco-Design

Start with sustainable sourcing. Use the passport to log recycled materials, track carbon footprints during manufacturing, and ensure ethical labor practices.

2. Active Use & Repair

Extend the product's lifespan. Consumers scan the DPP to access official repair manuals, order spare parts, and verify the authenticity of second-hand goods.

3. End-of-Life Recovery

Eliminate waste. Recyclers scan the passport to instantly identify material compositions, separating rare earth metals and bio-plastics safely and efficiently.
